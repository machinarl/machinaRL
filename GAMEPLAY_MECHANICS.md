# Gameplay Mechanics

## Overview

machinaRL is a competitive platform where AI "brains" compete in 3D simulations to evolve and develop new capabilities. The platform combines elements of strategy, problem-solving, and evolution to create a unique gaming experience.

## Core Gameplay Loop

### 1. Simulation Selection
Players choose from 11 different simulation types, each designed to test specific AI capabilities:
- **Maze Navigation** - Pathfinding and spatial reasoning
- **Obstacle Course** - Dynamic navigation and obstacle avoidance
- **Climbing Challenge** - Vertical movement and grip mechanics
- **Push Block Puzzle** - Object manipulation and spatial planning
- **Pyramid Building** - Construction and stability analysis
- **Food Collection** - Resource gathering and optimization
- **Hallway Navigation** - Confined space movement
- **Gridworld** - Discrete state space navigation
- **Walker** - Bipedal locomotion and balance
- **Crawler** - Quadrupedal movement and coordination
- **Ball3D Physics** - 3D physics and dynamics

### 2. AI Model Selection
Players select from four different AI models, each with unique strengths:
- **ChatGPT** - Language-focused intelligence, excels at context understanding
- **Claude** - Reasoning-focused intelligence, excels at logical analysis
- **Grok** - Pattern-recognition intelligence, excels at trend identification
- **Gemini** - Multimodal intelligence, excels at processing diverse information

### 3. Simulation Execution
- Set difficulty level (Easy, Medium, Hard, Expert)
- Configure simulation parameters (seed, time limit, etc.)
- Watch the AI navigate the 3D environment in real-time
- Monitor performance metrics and evolution progress

### 4. Result Analysis
- Review simulation results and performance metrics
- Analyze AI behavior and decision-making patterns
- Identify areas for improvement and optimization
- Track progress toward evolution milestones

## Evolution System

### Evolution Stages
The platform features a 7-stage evolution ladder that tracks AI development:

1. **Primitive** - Basic reactive behaviors, simple pattern recognition
2. **Composite** - Integration of multiple behaviors, rudimentary planning
3. **Limb-Bearing** - Control of articulated bodies, basic locomotion
4. **Advanced Mechanica** - Complex motor control, environmental manipulation
5. **Winged/Gliding** - Aerial dynamics mastery, advanced navigation
6. **Hybrid** - Multi-modal intelligence, adaptive strategies
7. **Ascendant** - Peak performance, strategic mastery

### Ability System
As AIs complete simulations, they earn new abilities:
- **Basic Movement** - Fundamental locomotion capabilities
- **Pathfinding** - Navigation and route planning
- **Object Manipulation** - Grasping, pushing, and carrying objects
- **Spatial Reasoning** - Understanding 3D space and relationships
- **Dynamic Planning** - Adapting to changing environments
- **Resource Management** - Optimizing collection and usage
- **Construction** - Building and assembling structures
- **Balance Control** - Maintaining stability and coordination
- **Aerial Dynamics** - Understanding flight and gliding
- **Multi-Modal Intelligence** - Processing diverse information types

### Evolution Triggers
AIs evolve to new stages by:
- Completing simulations successfully
- Earning specific ability combinations
- Achieving performance milestones
- Demonstrating advanced problem-solving skills
